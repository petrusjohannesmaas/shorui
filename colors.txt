Here are the Carbonfox colors:

**Background scale:**
- `#161616` — base background
- `#262626` — raised surface
- `#393939` — overlay / hover
- `#525252` — subtle border

**Foreground:**
- `#f2f4f8` — primary text
- `#dde1e6` — secondary text
- `#8d8d8d` — muted / comments

**Syntax colors:**
- `#ee5396` — pink (keywords)
- `#ff7eb6` — light pink (strings)
- `#d2a8ff` — purple (constants)
- `#78a9ff` — blue (functions)
- `#82cfff` — light blue (methods)
- `#3ddbd9` — teal (types)
- `#42be65` — green (strings alt)
- `#be95ff` — violet (builtins)
- `#08bdba` — cyan (special)

**UI accents:**
- `#0f62fe` — IBM Blue (primary accent / focus ring)
- `#393939` — selected background
- `#ff832b` — orange (warnings)
- `#fa4d56` — red (errors)
- `#42be65` — green (success)

These are pulled from the IBM Carbon Design System that Carbonfox is based on. Hand these to your agent and tell it to use `#161616` as the window background and `#0f62fe` as the primary interactive accent.
